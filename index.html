— Questions

Things you might be wondering.

Will Mirra track me, sell my data, or send me notifications I didn't ask for?+
No. Mirra collects no personal data beyond what's stored locally on your device. We don't sell, share, or process anything to third parties. Notifications are opt-in and limited to one per day at most. We've been audited by the Open Privacy Alliance every year since 2023.
Is the science behind it actually real?+
The breathwork patterns we use are drawn from established protocols (resonant breathing, box breathing, 4-7-8) with decades of published research. We're cautious about overclaiming — Mirra is a comfort, not a clinical tool. If you're struggling with persistent insomnia, please speak with a doctor.
Can I use it on my Apple Watch without my phone?+
Yes. Mirra runs fully on watchOS 10+ and works offline once content is downloaded. Breathwork sessions, mood check-ins, and soundscape playback are all available without your phone nearby.
What's the difference between Mirra and other sleep apps?+
Most apps in this category are built around streaks, gamification, or anxiety-inducing dashboards. We deliberately avoid all three. Mirra is designed to be used less over time, not more — the goal is to disappear into your evening, not become part of it.
Who made Mirra?+
A team of eleven, based in Brighton (UK) and Kyoto. We were previously at the BBC, Headspace, and Studio Ghibli's music department. Our founder, Imogen Frye, started Mirra after her own sleep collapsed during a year of grief in 2021.
Refund policy?+
If Mirra isn't for you, email us within 60 days of subscribing and we'll refund you in full, no questions. We mean this.
